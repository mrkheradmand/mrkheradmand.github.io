About

What I do

I have been building web applications since 2007 — first as a freelance PHP developer, and now in Python and Django as co-founder of MassiveCoda, where I own the backend: data modelling, business logic, integrations and the release cycle.

Alongside that, I have spent more than a decade at Subhosting on the support desk and billing — diagnosing real customer problems across servers, DNS, deployments and billing systems. Living with production issues every day changes how you write code. You think harder about failure states, logging, and the person who has to support the thing at 9am on a Monday.

Most recently I have been working on applying AI to development: integrating LLM APIs into product features, and using AI coding assistants as a normal part of how I build and review code.
